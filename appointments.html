Appointments

Information about booking, changing and cancelling appointments at Bodowen Surgery.

Booking an Appointment

Appointments can be booked by telephone, in person or through available online services.

Patients will be offered the most appropriate appointment according to clinical need.

Urgent Same-Day Care

Urgent appointments are available for patients with conditions requiring assessment on the same day.

Routine Appointments

  • GP Consultations
  • Advanced Clinical Practitioner Clinics
  • Practice Nurse Appointments
  • Healthcare Assistant Clinics
  • Long-Term Condition Reviews

Home Visits

Home visits are available for housebound patients who are medically unable to attend the surgery.
