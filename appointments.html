Appointments at Bodowen Surgery

Information about booking, changing and cancelling appointments at Bodowen Surgery.

Booking Appointments

Appointments may be booked by telephone, in person at reception or through available online services.

Urgent Same-Day Care

Same-day appointments are available for urgent problems according to clinical need.

Routine Appointments

Routine GP, ACP, Practice Nurse and Healthcare Assistant appointments can be booked in advance.

Home Visits

Home visits are intended for patients who are genuinely housebound or medically unable to attend the surgery.

Minor Illness Clinic

Assessment and treatment of common conditions including sore throats, ear problems, skin conditions and minor infections.

Cancel An Appointment

If you no longer require your appointment, please contact us as soon as possible so the appointment can be offered to another patient.

Need Medical Advice When We Are Closed?

Contact NHS 111 Wales for urgent medical advice outside normal surgery hours.
